Methodology — PF&DS Team, APUIAML
APUIAML, under the directions of the Principal Secretary, MA&UD and the Commissioner & Director of Municipal Administration (C&DMA), Government of Andhra Pradesh, has assessed the borrowing capacity of 123 Urban Local Bodies across the State. Drawing on multi-year historical financial data, the PF&DS team has applied a structured Financial Operating Plan (FOP) methodology and projected revenues and expenditures on conservative, bankable assumptions. The assessment evaluates each ULB's fiscal headroom against market-standard financial benchmarks and prudential debt-servicing norms to arrive at the maximum permissible borrowing limit for each ULB under four loan tenor scenarios.

For detailed analysis or ULB-specific queries, contact the PF&DS Team: pfs@apurban.in
